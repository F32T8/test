Bad way to end a holiday break, but things could be worse. The sickness is coming in clutch as a good excuse for laziness, though. A quiet day at home, as usual. Still reflecting on the past year. I think my biggest revelation is that shared values are more important than shared beliefs. It may be a compromise, but values are ultimately what’s important. I care less if you share my particular strain of skepticism and more if you value the things that I value. People over profit. Human rights. Leftist values. Etc. Skepticism is still important, but maybe it’s a little further down the list than I used to think. The continual slip into individual realities is damaging to the collective. And it makes trying to correct every little thing impossible. Limited energy. Probably should focus on what’s most important. (And be a bit more kind.) A lot of things are bullshit. But some of it is much more harmful than others. Again, this can be considered a compromise. If things weren’t so volatile, maybe the playing field would be more level. But as is, you’ve gotta pick your battles. And that changes the way I approach things, in a big way. In other news, this site is now listed on textonly.website, which is a collection of personal websites with a focus on minimalism. I’m a big fan of this effort, but it may be worth disclaiming that not every site listed has my endorsement. (There’s at least one Christian crypto chud polluting the waters, unfortunately.) My other favorite collection of personal sites is webring.xxiivv.com. Maybe I’ll try to get listed there once this place is more built out. --- 2024-12-28 SATURDAY I have the self-awareness but lack the discipline. Time slips by. So it goes. This week has been a wash. It’s time for rest, but it still feels kinda bad to have done so little. Maybe 2025 will be different. More focus. More art. More writing. More exercise. We’ll see. Looking back, I didn’t make very much this year. I’ve also continued to disappear from the internet. The fact that those things are related makes me a little uncomfortable. But why make something if you’re not going to show it to people? I’m sure there are good answers. It also feels surprisingly comfortable to slip further away. Into smaller, quieter spaces. And even spending some time offline altogether. I do miss seeing what certain people are up to and being exposed to fragments of The Culture. But also, fuck it, man. Not worth the compromise. Not worth the hassle. And my own attempts at building small communities have largely failed. Understandable. I’m not too bitter about it. Things are just broken up and the Powers That Be have poisoned the biggest pieces. I will continue having my own website(s) and advocating for the return to the personal web. This one is my first time going semi-anonymous. I’ve spent most of my online life going by my whole-ass name. Giving namelessness a try feels nice. And good timing, considering current events. More later. Thanks for reading. Broadcasting from a suburb in the heart of America. Started in December 2024. This site is (mostly) plain text. There's a bit of HTML/CSS to wrap the text, make the line height a little more comfortable, and allow for dark mode. The goal is to keep things as simple as possible and to work within restraints. Largely inspired by textonly.website (which is maintained by koray.er), minim.blog, divacorp.net, and the idea of keeping a digital journal using a .txt file. Lastly, this site should not be discoverable via search engines and has a robots.txt file that blocks (or discourages) crawlers. The only way anyone should be able to find this site is via a direct link or through textonly.website. — — — — — — [ Fix your heart or die. ]
